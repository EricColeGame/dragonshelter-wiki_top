1. Launch your game client and load your active save file.
2. Open the main **Settings** or **Pause Menu** (typically accessed via the Escape key on PC or the Options button on controllers).

## Frequently Asked Questions About Dragon Shelter Codes

### Are dragon shelter codes case-sensitive?
Yes, in nearly all modern PC and console titles, promotional keys must match the exact capitalization shown in official announcements. If an active string uses capital letters, entering it entirely in lowercase will trigger an "invalid code" alert. For best results, highlight the string and paste it straight into the redemption box to avoid character mistakes.

### How often do developers publish new dragon shelter codes?
Developers typically distribute new **dragon shelter codes** around major milestone achievements, such as reaching a target number of community wishlists, launching major content expansions, or celebrating holiday events. During quiet development cycles, code drops may become less frequent, making it essential to redeem active promotions before they hit their expiration deadlines.

### Can expired codes ever be reactivated later?
Generally, no. Once a promotional key has reached its programmed cutoff date, server authentication systems reject subsequent redemption attempts. Occasionally, developers may re-issue popular rewards under a brand-new code during anniversary festivals, but legacy strings rarely return to active status.

### Do promotional codes work across all game platforms?
Cross-platform availability depends on whether the title features unified cloud saves or platform-locked licensing. While general promotional bundles usually deploy across all operating systems simultaneously, platform-exclusive gifts (such as console-specific launch items) will only function on their designated storefront. Always check the release notes accompanying the reward to ensure compatibility with your system.
