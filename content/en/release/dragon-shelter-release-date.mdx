## Core Gameplay Mechanics: What to Expect at Launch

At its core, *Dragon Shelter* blends the tranquil rhythms of pastoral life with the wonder of high-fantasy stewardship. Players take on the role of a determined protagonist dedicated to rebuilding an abandoned haven where humans and dragons once thrived side by side. Instead of treating creatures merely as combat pets or livestock, the game frames each dragon species as a partner in community renewal.

### 1. Sanctuary Reconstruction and Crafting
The surrounding region begins in disrepair, overgrown with weeds, ruined stone shelters, and blocked mountain paths. Players clear debris, collect timber and stone, and erect functional outbuildings. As your sanctuary expands, you unlock specialized facilities such as nurseries, elemental roosts, apothecary tables, and visitor kiosks.

### 2. Elemental Dragon Care and Abilities
Every dragon rescued boasts distinct elemental affinities—such as flame, frost, verdant growth, and gusting wind. Nurturing these creatures involves understanding their individual dietary preferences, shelter needs, and play styles. In turn, happy dragons assist with day-to-day operations:

- **Fire Dragons:** Stoke forge fires, clear hardened crystal barriers, and warm soil during chilly seasons.
- **Water/Frost Dragons:** Irrigate expansive farm plots, extinguish brushfires, and preserve perishable crops.
- **Flora Dragons:** Accelerate seed sprouting, rejuvenate depleted soil patches, and discover wild seeds.
- **Gale Dragons:** Power grain windmills, disperse pest clouds, and carry resources across great distances.
