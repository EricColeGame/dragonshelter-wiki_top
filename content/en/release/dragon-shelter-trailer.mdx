## Actionable Tips for Prospective Dragon Keepers

While awaiting the full launch, aspiring caretakers can adopt several smart habits to prepare their mindset for the shelter management loop shown in the trailers:

1. **Prioritize Habitat Diversity Over Pure Size**: The footage reveals that crowded enclosures depress companion mood. Planning modular enclosures with water ponds, shaded perches, and open basking spots will make dragon integration much smoother.
2. **Stockpile Native Botanicals Early**: Healing injured dragons requires varied poultices and specialized foods. Gathering a reserve of wild herbs and seeds during your first seasonal cycle will prevent shortages when unexpected rescue missions arise.
3. **Foster Elemental Balance**: Do not focus exclusively on one dragon breed. Cultivating a diverse team with fire, water, earth, and wind affinities ensures you have the necessary elemental abilities to overcome any environmental roadblock in the wild.
4. **Invest in Village Relationships**: Townsfolk are not just quest givers; they supply upgraded tools and building blueprints that directly increase your shelter's operational efficiency.

By focusing on balance, patience, and thoughtful sanctuary layout, you will be fully prepared to build a thriving haven the moment you set foot in the valley.
