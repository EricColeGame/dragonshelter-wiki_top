## Seasonal Crop Planning and Soil Management

Seasons influence your farming strategy directly. Temperature shifts, drought periods, and torrential rains alter how frequently you must attend to your plots. Implementing a structured seasonal rotation guarantees continuous income and an uninterrupted harvest pipeline.

Player experience suggests that over-farming identical plots without compost replenishment leads to soil degradation. After every two cycles of intensive crops like Star Blooms, plant a cover crop or apply organic compost to reset soil vitality.

```
Spring: High Rainfall -> Focus on fast root vegetables and foundational grains
Summer: High Sunlight -> Prioritize heat-loving elemental peppers and melons
Autumn: Balanced Winds -> Harvest bulk star grains, squashes, and alchemy herbs
Winter: Freezing Ground -> Transition to greenhouse farming and cold-resistant ferns
```

| Season | Optimal Crops | Weather Hazards | Preventive Countermeasure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spring** | Sunleaf Wheat, Glowroot Tubers | Flash flooding, muddy soil | Dig drainage trenches around field edges |
| **Summer** | Ember Peppers, Dew Melons | Heatwaves, dried plots | Assign Water Drakes; apply straw mulch |
| **Autumn** | Star Bloom, Hearth Pumpkins | Heavy winds, falling debris | Erect windbreak fences; reinforce trellis plots |
| **Winter** | Frost Fern, Snow Moss | Frozen topsoil, blizzards | Utilize Fire Drake warming pens or glasshouses |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How often should I refer to a dragon shelter farming guide when expanding my shelter?
It is best to review farming benchmarks whenever you unlock a new elemental dragon habitat or discover new seed tiers. Adjusting your layout and crop distributions early saves substantial gold and remodeling effort later.

### Can dragons damage crops if they run out of stamina?
Community reports suggest that dragons with zero stamina or low happiness may become disobedient. While they rarely destroy mature crops permanently, their failure to water or warm plots can delay harvest windows by an entire day cycle.

### What is the most profitable crop for beginner farmers?
In the early game, Sunleaf Wheat combined with Dew Melons provides the most stable returns. Sunleaf Wheat grows quickly with minimal maintenance, while Dew Melons regrow multiple times without requiring repurchase of seeds, minimizing early overhead costs.

### Do fertilizers stack with dragon elemental auras?
Yes. Using high-grade compost alongside a Nature Drake's natural growth aura yields compounding bonuses, significantly increasing your chances of harvesting rare Star-grade crops.
