## Step-by-Step Guide: Dragon Shelter How to Make Bread

Executing the complete recipe requires managing four distinct phases: cultivation, processing, preparation, and baking. Following a systematic approach ensures you avoid burned batches or wasted ingredients.

```
[Harvest Wheat] ➔ [Mill into Fine Flour] ➔ [Mix Dough with Water] ➔ [Bake in Heated Oven]
```

### Step 1: Cultivating and Harvesting Wheat

Wheat is the foundational grain for all traditional baking recipes. You can acquire your first pouch of wheat seeds from local merchants or by foraging wild grasses around the valley borders. 

1. **Clear the Ground**: Remove wild weeds, branches, and loose rocks using your starter tools.
2. **Till the Soil**: Equip your hoe and till a minimum grid of 3x3 tiles to make watering efficient.
3. **Sow the Seeds**: Plant your wheat seeds evenly across the tilled plots.
4. **Water Daily**: Ensure the soil remains damp every morning. Missing a watering day delays harvest cycles.
5. **Harvest with a Sickle**: When the stalks turn vibrant golden-amber, harvest the crop using your scythe or sickle.

### Step 2: Milling Grain into Fine Flour

Raw wheat cannot be placed directly into an oven. You must grind the dried sheaves into usable flour at your milling station or village windmill. Interacting with the grindstone brings up the processing interface where you can queue grain batches.

### Step 3: Mixing the Dough

At your indoor or outdoor kitchen counter, combine your milled flour with clean bucket water. Adding a dash of yeast or wild starter (available from forageable fruit ferments) creates standard dough balls ready for baking.

### Step 4: Firing the Oven

Light your oven using basic fuel such as branch bundles, dried logs, or charcoal. Place your prepared dough onto the stone hearth. Keep a close watch on the progress meter; pulling the loaf out too early yields undercooked dough, while leaving it too long results in ruined ash loaves.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Making Bread in Dragon Shelter

### What is the most common mistake when learning in dragon shelter how to make bread?
The most frequent mistake beginner players make is forgetting to manage fuel burn times. Leaving dough inside an unmonitored oven after the cooking bar fills leads directly to burnt ash loaves, destroying both your grain and your fuel.

### Where can I acquire more wheat seeds if local stores run out?
If the town merchant's stock is depleted, you can harvest wild grasses along the forest borders using a sickle. Wild grass drops seed bundles at a steady rate. Alternatively, completing early bulletin board favors for neighboring farmers frequently rewards multi-seed packs.

### Can helper dragons automate the bread making process?
Yes. As your shelter expands and your affinity with helper dragons grows, specific companions can be assigned tasks such as carrying water buckets from the well, turning the grindstone, or tending oven fires, freeing you to forage and explore.

### How long does finished bread stay fresh in your inventory?
Unlike raw meat or dairy products, baked bread items have an exceptionally long preservation timer. Storing finished loaves inside an insulated pantry bin prevents degradation indefinitely, making bread the ideal food reserve for long winter seasons.
