## Frequently Asked Questions About Dragon Shelter Dragon Breeding

### How do I unlock dragon shelter dragon breeding in the sanctuary?
Breeding becomes available after restoring the central nursery grove and completing the sanctuary elder's introductory lineage quest. Once you have built your first insulated nest and raised two compatible dragons to adulthood with maximum bond levels, the courtship interface unlocks automatically.

### Can two dragons of the same element produce a rare hybrid?
While two dragons of the same element generally produce offspring sharing that element, recessive mutations can still trigger rare scale colors or boosted work stats. However, unlocking dual-element hybrids requires pairing two distinct elemental lineages.

### How long does dragon shelter dragon breeding take from courtship to hatching?
In standard dragon shelter dragon breeding cycles, courtship takes between two to four in-game hours, while egg incubation ranges from 12 to 48 in-game hours depending on the egg's rarity and elemental classification. Upgraded incubators can significantly speed up this timeline.

### What should I do if my dragon egg shows signs of cold stress?
If an egg incubator indicator turns blue or begins shivering, add thermal fuel to the nursery hearth immediately or relocate the clutch into a Thermal Hearth Nest. Prolonged cold stress can delay hatching and lower your hatchling's starting health stats.
