## Frequently Asked Questions About Dragon Helpers

### How many dragon shelter dragon helpers can you have working at one time?
Active helper capacity depends directly on your shelter's core level and the number of certified nests built on your property. Early compounds support between two and three active companions, while fully upgraded sanctuaries can comfortably host and deploy over a dozen helpers across various outposts.

### What should I do if a dragon helper refuses to work?
If a companion stops working, check their happiness gauge, stamina meter, and habitat comfort level. Helpers refuse orders when their energy is depleted, when their nest is unkempt, or when they are hungry. Providing their favorite meal or scheduling a restful nap in the medical den usually resolves work strikes promptly.

### Can dragon helpers accidentally damage crops or farm infrastructure?
No, companions will not destroy crops or damage structures when performing assigned tasks. However, if an elemental mismatch occurs—such as a fire drake becoming startled near dry hay bales—temporary smoke hazards may develop, requiring a quick spray from an aquatic companion to clear the air.
