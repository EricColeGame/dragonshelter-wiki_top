## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which dragon shelter dragon types should beginners unlock first?
Early in your journey, focus primarily on rescuing and befriending Water- and Earth-type dragons, such as the Puddle Drake and Bramble Sprout. These companions automate the most stamina-draining tasks—watering crops and tilling tough soil—allowing you to allocate your daily energy toward exploration, foraging, and gathering rare materials.

### Can different dragon shelter dragon types share the same enclosure?
While certain docile breeds can share communal roaming yards, housing incompatible species in the exact same pen causes territorial stress. For instance, pairing a Fire dragon with an aquatic species inside a cramped stable will lower the happiness levels of both animals. For best results, use partitioned paddocks connected by open pastures.

### How do I cure an unhappy or sick dragon companion?
If a dragon refuses to work, inspect its status pane for negative mood modifiers. Common causes include uncleaned sleeping quarters, exposure to rain for fire types, or consecutive days of generic trough feeding. You can quickly restore morale by brewing specialty herbal remedies, hand-grooming their coats, and gifting their favorite artisanal treats.
