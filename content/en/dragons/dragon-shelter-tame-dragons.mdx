## Core Mechanics of Dragon Taming and Trust

Earning the devotion of a wild dragon requires understanding their emotional states and physiological needs. When exploring the wilderness in *Dragon Shelter*, tame dragons require more than simple food offerings; players must approach them with situational awareness. Wild dragons often suffer from lingering ailments, environmental stress, or plain exhaustion caused by corrupted corruption zones surrounding the valley.

Trust is represented by a progressive meter divided into four distinct emotional thresholds: Skittish, Curious, Receptive, and Bonded. Rushing toward a skittish creature will cause it to flee into dense brush, resetting its progress. Approaching in a slow crouch, presenting preferred treats, and paying close attention to visual body language will keep the interaction calm and positive.

```
Wild Encounter -> Read Body Language -> Present Calming Bait -> Gentle Interaction -> Sanctuary Transfer
```

## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Tame Your First Wild Dragon

Taming wild creatures demands deliberate preparation before stepping beyond the sanctuary gates. To succeed in *Dragon Shelter*, tame dragons by following a structured taming ritual that honors each creature's boundaries. Skipping key steps or attempting to rush through trust thresholds often leads to wasted bait and frightened beasts.

Community reports suggest stocking up on multiple types of forage treats before venturing into newly unlocked sub-regions. Certain high-tier drakes will refuse basic forage if they smell more suitable aromatic plants nearby.

```
Step 1: Scout & Observe -> Step 2: Prepare Bait -> Step 3: Gentle Approach -> Step 4: Medical Care -> Step 5: Welcome Home
```

1. **Scout the Habitat:** Locate the wild dragon during its natural active window (diurnal or nocturnal) and observe its current behavioral status without stepping into its awareness radius.
2. **Select the Proper Offering:** Inspect your foraging pouch for foods matching the target's elemental class. Never feed cold river plants to an ember drake.
3. **Approach at Low Profile:** Toggle to slow-walk mode. Stop immediately if the creature raises its crest, growls, or flares its wings.
4. **Deploy the Bait:** Place the treat on the ground halfway between you and the creature, then take two full steps backward to give it space.
5. **Tend to Injuries:** Once the creature feeds, inspect it for thorn scratches, parasitic spores, or exhaustion. Apply crafted herbal salves to patch its wounds.
6. **Escort to Habitat:** Guide the calm dragon back toward your homestead, ensuring it rests whenever its stamina indicator dips low.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can dragons ever permanently abandon your sanctuary?
No, dragons do not permanently leave your care if their happiness drops to zero. However, they will refuse to work, will sleep outside their assigned pens, and will not produce rare shedding materials until you improve their living conditions and soothe their distress with favored treats.

### How do seasonal weather changes affect dragon moods?
Each season introduces shifts that benefit some species while stressing others. Cold winter blizzards lower the mood of Fire and Nature dragons unless you upgrade their shelters with thermal insulation, whereas Ice and Wind dragons experience morale and speed bonuses during freezing months.

### What is the most efficient way in Dragon Shelter to tame dragons that are territorial?
Territorial dragons require sensory masking before you attempt an approach. Crafting herbal scent-masking pouches allows you to enter their nesting perimeter without triggering an aggressive threat display. Once close, placing bait and retreating out of direct sightlines allows them to eat without feeling cornered.

### How many dragons can live in the sanctuary simultaneously?
Sanctuary capacity depends entirely on your current estate rank and available shelter infrastructure. While you start with space for only two companions, upgrading your central lodge and clearing wild parcels lets you support over a dozen unique drakes across various tailored biomes.
